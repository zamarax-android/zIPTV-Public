# zIPTV for Android

Ultra-fast, simple and powerful IPTV app for Android — the Android edition of
[zIPTV for Windows](https://github.com/zamarax-windows/zIPTV).

- Import your IPTV channels from any source (M3U file, M3U link, Xtream, Stalker portal)
- Full TiviMate-style EPG guide with catchup playback and reminder notifications
- Player for every taste: an embedded player docked at the top of the app, a draggable floating mini player (Undock), or fully immersive fullscreen — each with on-video close/expand controls
- Background and scheduled recordings (exact alarms fire them even when the app is closed; a foreground notification keeps them alive with the screen off)
- Favorites, custom channels, sharing, re-stream to friends/other devices
- Trakt.tv and Simkl integrations
- Configurable themes, Android TV (D-pad) support
- Super low RAM usage, crazy speeds, and instant search

## Download

Grab `zIPTV_<version>_universal.apk` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/zamarax-android/zIPTV-Public/releases/latest)
and sideload it (allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/file manager
when prompted).

Requirements: Android 7.0+ (API 24+). The APK is universal (arm64-v8a,
armeabi-v7a, x86, x86_64).

## Getting started

1. Launch zIPTV and open **Settings**
2. Add a source: an **M3U link**, a local **M3U file**, or your **Xtream**
   credentials
3. Close the settings dialog and start watching

Long-press a channel tile or a guide program for the context menu (the
equivalent of right-click on the desktop app).
